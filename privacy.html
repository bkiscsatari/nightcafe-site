Privacy Policy

The Night Café / Viral Content Engine · Last updated: 26 August 2026

The "Viral Content Engine" is a private, personal-use application operated by the owner of The Night Café YouTube channel. Its sole purpose is to automate the production and uploading of the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts.

What data the application handles

  • The application is used exclusively by its owner. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, any other person.
  • It does not collect, store, process, sell, or share personal data of any third party.
  • Google account access (the youtube.upload permission) is used solely to upload videos to the owner's own channel. The resulting credentials are stored privately on the owner's own infrastructure and are never shared.
  • The application reads only publicly available platform data (such as public video statistics) through official APIs.

Cookies and tracking

This website is a static page. It sets no cookies and contains no analytics or tracking.
